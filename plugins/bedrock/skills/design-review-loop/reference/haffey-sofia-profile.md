# Haffey / SOFIA Profile

This adapter may bind the portable review contract to a live, accepted SOFIA recipe/kernel. It carries no private tracker state, repository path, prompt snapshot, or claim that the machinery is currently available.

Before use, resolve and record from the live accepted SOFIA contract:

1. recipe and kernel identifiers and immutable versions;
2. supported portable schema versions and any documented field mapping;
3. stance charters, arbitration capability, durable ledger, identity/lifecycle gates, cancellation, budgets, and evidence export;
4. exact command/API entrypoint and validator profile;
5. authority boundary proving Bedrock actors remain actors while SOFIA owns recipe sequencing, kernel execution, and control-plane state.

If any required capability is absent, incompatible, proposed rather than accepted, or cannot emit distributable evidence, downgrade to multi-perspective/direct review or stop. Do not fill gaps with copied SOFIA prompts, hand-evaluated gates, tracker assertions, or an emulated loop. The installed contract may name the adapter boundary but must not embed private locations, tickets, credentials, or corpus content.

Only a fresh manifest proving that the resolved recipe/kernel and all required gates executed may support a SOFIA runner-backed convergence claim.
